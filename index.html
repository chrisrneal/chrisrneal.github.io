How I work

Plain language, short cycles, and something real to look at early.

  1. We talk it through

    You explain the problem in your own words. I ask questions until I can say it back clearly.

  2. You see it working fast

    I use AI tools to build a working version quickly, so you react to a real app, not a slide deck.

  3. We refine together

    You tell me what to change. We repeat in short rounds until it fits how your team actually works.

  4. It's yours to run

    You get a live app you can open from any browser, and a clear explanation of how it all fits together.
